# A little room for big ideas.

Welcome to **MDcard** — a calm place to write, think, and make something worth sharing.

> Great things start with a few words.

## Make it yours

- Write in **Markdown**, see it come to life.
- Switch between source, split view, and reading.
  - Your words stay yours: plain UTF-8 files.
  - Works on Windows and Linux.

## Everything in its place

| Feature | A little detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| Live preview | Headings, lists, quotes and tables |
